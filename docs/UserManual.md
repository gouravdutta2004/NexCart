# User Manual & Operator Guide
## NexCart — Intelligent Supermarket Automation System

### 1. Introduction
Welcome to **NexCart**, an automated enterprise platform designed for supermarkets, grocery stores, and hypermarkets. This manual provides role-specific instructions for Customers, Cashiers, Inventory Managers, and Administrators.

---

### 2. Role-Based Operator Guides

#### 2.1 Customer Guide
- **Account Registration:** Click **Register** on the top navigation bar, provide your name, email, and password.
- **Product Catalog & Filters:** Filter products by categories, search by keyword, or view discounted clearance items.
- **Self-Checkout Mode:** When in-store, switch to **Self-Checkout Mode**. Use the integrated barcode scanner simulation or hardware scanner to add items directly to your personal cart. Proceed to instant simulated checkout and download your digital PDF invoice.

#### 2.2 Cashier POS Operator Guide
- **Login:** Log in with your Cashier credentials. You will be routed directly to the full-width POS Billing Terminal.
- **Scanning Items:** Focus is automatically placed on the **Barcode Input** field. Scan items using a USB/Bluetooth barcode scanner or type the numeric barcode and press `Enter`.
- **Quantity Adjustments:** Use `+` and `-` buttons or keypad shortcuts to adjust line item counts.
- **Holding Baskets:** If a customer needs to fetch an extra item, click **Hold Cart** (`F7`) to store their basket and serve the next customer. Use **Recall Cart** to restore it.
- **Payment & Printing:** Click **Pay Now** (`F12`), select payment method (Cash/Card/UPI), input tendered cash to calculate change, and click **Finalize Sale**. The digital invoice and thermal slip preview appear immediately.

#### 2.3 Inventory Manager Guide
- **Stock Overview:** Access the **Inventory Dashboard** to view total SKUs, total on-hand inventory, and valuation.
- **Expiry Radar:** Review the **Expiring Items** table. Items flagged in red are expiring within 7 days.
- **Applying Clearance Markdowns:** Click **Apply Clearance Discount** to automatically create discounted clearance rules for near-expiry batches.
- **Purchase Orders:** Generate new purchase orders for low-stock items and mark items as "Received" when shipments arrive, which automatically initializes new FEFO stock batches.

#### 2.4 Administrator Guide
- **Executive Analytics:** Monitor real-time Gross Merchandise Value (GMV), net margin, customer count, and average order value.
- **Demand Forecasting:** Review the 7-day and 30-day moving-average demand predictions to optimize supplier orders.
- **Employee Management:** Create, update, or deactivate employee logins and change assigned roles.
- **Audit Logs:** Filter audit entries by module, action, or date range to inspect who made modifications to prices, stocks, or permissions.
